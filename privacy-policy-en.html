PRIVACY AND POLICY

Last Updated: February 3, 2026  |  Version: 1.0.0

Welcome to our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data.

1. Data Collection

  • Account Information: When you log in via Google Sign-In, we only collect your user ID, name, and email address for identification purposes.
  • App Usage Data: This includes your bookmarked articles and search history.

2. Data Storage (SQLite)

  • All personalization data, saved news, and search history are stored directly on your device using a local database (SQLite). We do not upload this data to our servers unless explicitly permitted by you or for account synchronization.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display ads. These services may collect and use data (such as device Ad ID) to display ads tailored to your interests.
  • News Sources: The app displays content from online newspapers. By accessing original links, you are subject to the privacy policies of each website.

4. User Rights

  • You can clear all locally stored data by clearing the app data in your phone's settings.
  • You may request the deletion of your account and related data at any time via the contact section within the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at: 2usoft.lql@gmail.com.
